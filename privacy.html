Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

us-stocks-uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Stockwise YouTube channel (@StockwiseHQ) to upload that channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions.

Data the tool accesses

The tool uses the YouTube Data API with the channel owner's authorization to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channel. It does not access any other person's Google or YouTube data.

Data collection and sharing

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal information from anyone. The authorization token is kept on the owner's own computer and is not transferred to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the About page of the Stockwise channel on YouTube.
